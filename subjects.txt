Can I Ignore a Small Typo in My Name on a Plane Ticket?
  Separate a simple misspelling from an actual name change — airlines handle
  those differently. Scenario candidates: one letter wrong, first and middle
  names swapped, a missing middle name, a married-name mismatch.

Can I Ignore a Passport That Expires in Less Than 6 Months?
  Six months is not a universal rule; the validity a destination requires
  varies. The hook is "it hasn't expired, so why can't I fly?"

Can I Ignore a Damaged Passport?
  A separate question from expiry — physical condition alone can matter.
  Scenario candidates: water damage, a torn page, a damaged photo page, a
  damaged cover, loose binding.

Can I Ignore a Short Layover Between Flights?
  Same ticket versus separate tickets changes the answer completely, as does
  whether immigration, a security re-screen or a terminal change is involved.

Can I Ignore Signs of Bed Bugs in a Hotel Room?
  Scenario candidates: dark spots on the mattress seam, blood stains on the
  sheets, shed skins, a live insect. What makes this different from a household
  pest page is that it can follow you home in the luggage.

Can I Ignore a Hotel Room That Smells Like Mold?
  Scenario candidates: musty on opening the door, only when the AC runs, only
  in the bathroom. Different search intent from the existing Home mold page —
  the action here is whether to ask for another room.

Can I Ignore a Crack or Broken Wheel on My Suitcase?
  A spectrum rather than one answer. Scenario candidates: a hairline surface
  crack, one wobbling wheel, a zip that barely closes, a broken frame.

Can I Ignore a Scratch on a Rental Car?
  A money question rather than a safety one, which is what makes it different
  from the existing car pages. Scenario candidates: a small scratch, a curbed
  wheel, a glass chip, a bumper scuff, damage you are not sure predates you.

Can I Ignore a Boarding Pass That Won't Load on My Phone?
  Cover the alternatives that actually exist: the airport counter, a kiosk, a
  printed pass, a dead battery at the gate.

Can I Ignore the Wrong Destination on My Baggage Tag?
  The related question people ask is whether a bag is checked through to the
  final destination and whether it has to be re-collected at a connection.
