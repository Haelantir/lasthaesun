Can I Ignore Water Left in the Washing Machine Detergent Drawer?
  A small pool of water sits in the detergent dispenser drawer after a wash cycle finishes, not during it. Reader wants to know if this is normal residual moisture or a sign of a clog/drain problem. Separate "a few drops, common on HE machines" from "standing water every single wash" or "water there before the cycle even starts."

Can I Ignore a Hollow-Sounding Floor Tile?
  A homeowner walks on or taps a tile floor and one tile (or a small area) sounds hollow/tinny compared to the rest, but nothing is visibly cracked or loose yet. Reader wants to know if this means the tile has debonded from the subfloor and needs to be pulled up, or if some hollow sound is normal and can be watched. Separate "one isolated tile, sound only" from "spreading to neighboring tiles" or "tile is also cracking, lifting, or moving underfoot."
