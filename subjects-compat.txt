Steam Mop on Vinyl Plank Flooring
  LVP is marketed as fully waterproof, so buyers assume steam is automatically safe. Manufacturer allowances vary by brand and product line; check whether steam heat specifically (not just standing water) is approved, and what it can do at the seams over time. A close sibling, steam-mop--laminate-flooring, already ships and reached a different answer because laminate's core is not waterproof — LVP's marketing claim is the whole reason this is a separate question.

Drywall Anchors in Plaster Walls
  Older homes with plaster-and-lath or plaster-on-masonry walls. Plaster is harder and more brittle than drywall, and anchors designed for drywall's fibrous panel can crack plaster or fail to grip. Cover how the installation approach differs (pilot hole, anchor type) rather than just "it doesn't work."

Magic Eraser on Car Paint
  Melamine foam is mildly abrasive. The question is whether it can dull or scratch clear coat, especially with repeated use or on clear coat that is already thin or worn.
